BaymaXX Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

BaymaXX is a private assistant with a single user, its owner. This policy describes exactly what BaymaXX does with that person's data. BaymaXX has no other users, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or rents data.

Data BaymaXX uses

  • Wearable data, only for the data types the owner approves on the provider's consent screen: daily sleep, sleep periods, readiness, activity, heart rate, blood-oxygen (SpO2), stress, resilience, cardiovascular measures, VO2 max, workouts, sessions, tags, rest-mode periods and sleep-time recommendations. BaymaXX does not request the email address or the personal-information (age, height, weight, sex) data types.
  • Things the owner tells BaymaXX, such as habits, goals, symptoms, feedback and how a suggestion worked out.
  • Records BaymaXX derives: personal baselines, trends, observations, notes of past health conversations, and suggestions it made, with their reasons.
  • Authorization tokens for the wearable account.

Where it is stored

All of the above is stored only on the owner's own Mac, in a private folder outside any code repository. Health values, conversation notes and tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the files are readable only by the owner's macOS account. The encryption key is kept on the same Mac. That protects against accidental exposure (copies, backups, logs) but not against malicious software running under the owner's account, so the Mac should have disk encryption (FileVault) turned on.

BaymaXX also keeps local diagnostic logs on the same Mac, which can include conversation transcripts and health answers. These logs are readable only by the owner's account. Passwords, tokens, client secrets and keys are removed from them before they are written.

How it is used

Only to answer the owner's questions, describe trends compared with the owner's own history, add a short health note to the owner's morning briefing when something meaningful changes, and remember context the owner asked it to remember. Data is not used for advertising, profiling for others or model training by BaymaXX, and it is not used to make decisions about anyone.

Services that receive data

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Wearable provider's API Authorization requests and data requests for the owner's own account To read the data the owner authorized
Google Gemini API, with audio carried over LiveKit Cloud The owner's spoken conversation with BaymaXX, including any health answers spoken during it (summaries and figures, not full data histories) BaymaXX's voice runs on Google's realtime speech model
U.S. National Library of Medicine (PubMed) General topic keywords only, such as "sleep" or "caffeine". Never the owner's measurements, words or identity. To find published research when the owner asks for evidence
OpenAI API (optional for health features, off by default) If the owner enables it, a short summary of computed health figures (no raw data, no identity) To phrase an explanation more naturally

Those services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. BaymaXX does not share data with anyone else.

Retention

  • Wearable data, derived records and notes are kept on the Mac until the owner deletes them.
  • Reported symptoms are treated as expired after 30 days and general feedback after a year. Expired notes are no longer used but stay in local storage until deleted.
  • Cached research results contain only public article details and are refreshed after 30 days.
  • Diagnostic logs are kept until the owner deletes them. They are not rotated automatically.

Revoking consent and deleting data

  • Disconnect: BaymaXX revokes its access token with the wearable provider and deletes the stored tokens. The owner can also remove the application from the provider's developer portal at any time.
  • Delete wearable data: removes all imported wearable data, baselines and observations, keeping the owner's notes and conversation history.
  • Delete everything: removes all health data, notes, tokens and the encryption key from the Mac.
  • Deleting local logs removes any transcripts in them.

Contact preferences

BaymaXX never sends marketing or unsolicited messages. Health notes appear only inside the owner's own BaymaXX conversations and briefings, and the owner can mute any measurement or turn the health features off.

Children

BaymaXX is not intended for or used by children.

Changes

If BaymaXX's data handling changes, this page will be updated and its effective date changed.
