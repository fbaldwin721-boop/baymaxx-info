BaymaXX Privacy Terms

BaymaXX

A private, single-user personal assistant that runs on its owner's Mac.

BaymaXX is a personal project. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has exactly one user: the person who built it. It cannot be downloaded, installed or signed up for.

Health features

With the owner's explicit authorization, BaymaXX reads the owner's own wearable sleep and recovery data through the provider's official API. It uses that data to answer the owner's questions, describe trends compared with the owner's own history, and share general wellness information from public-health sources and published research.

BaymaXX does not diagnose conditions or give medical advice, and it is not a medical device.
